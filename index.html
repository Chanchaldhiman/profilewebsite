About

How I work

I work at the seam between hardware, software, and the system around both. Most of what I've shipped follows the same arc: model the behaviour first (MATLAB/Simulink, AMESim), prove it before hardware exists (HIL/SIL), then carry it to production with the traceability and safety documentation to survive an audit.

The graduate work behind that — Model Predictive Control, battery sizing optimisation, ICE/BEV/PHEV trade studies — still earns its keep daily. And because a fleet in the field generates more truth than any test plan, I build the Python/Azure/Power BI pipelines to listen to it.
